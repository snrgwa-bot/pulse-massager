Frequently asked questions

Everything about using PULSE, PULSE Pro and your privacy. Still stuck? Contact support.

I don't feel any vibration.

PULSE needs an iPhone with a Taptic Engine. Check that Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics is on. Low Power Mode can weaken haptics. The screen stays on during a session; vibration stops if you leave the app, because iOS doesn't allow haptics in the background.

How do I use it?

Tap the big button to start. Pick Soft, Medium or Hard, set the speed and an auto-off timer, then choose a mode from Relax, Deep or Mine. Hold the iPhone against your neck, shoulders or back.

Is PULSE a medical device?

No. It's a relaxation app. Stop if anything feels uncomfortable, and ask a doctor first if you are pregnant, have an implanted device or a medical condition.

What's free, and what's in Pro?

Free includes 4 modes (Steady, Waves, Breeze, Pulse), Soft and Medium intensity, and 5-minute sessions. Pro adds every mode including the guided Journey, Hard intensity, timers up to 60 minutes or no limit, and your own custom modes.

How much is Pro?

Weekly $2.99 (with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers), Yearly $19.99, or Lifetime $29.99 once. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Prices may vary by country.

How do I restore or cancel?

Restore on a new iPhone: Settings › Restore Purchases. Manage or cancel a subscription: Settings › Manage Subscription in the app, or your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

How do I switch between light and dark?

PULSE follows your iPhone's appearance. To choose Light or Dark just for PULSE, tap the appearance button at the top of the main screen.

Do you collect my data?

No. There's no account, no analytics and no ads. Your settings and custom modes stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a custom mode from Settings › My Modes, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Does PULSE work on iPad?

PULSE is designed for iPhone, which has the Taptic Engine that makes the vibration. It needs iOS 17 or later.

Does it keep vibrating if I lock the screen or switch apps?

No. Vibration stops when PULSE leaves the foreground, because iOS doesn't allow haptics in the background. That's why the screen stays on during a session.

What is the Journey?

Journey is a guided session in the Deep tab. It moves through six stages — Settle, Flow, Ease, Rise, Deep and Rest — each with its own rhythm, and takes about seven minutes. It's part of PULSE Pro.

Can I make my own rhythm?

Yes, with PULSE Pro. Open the Mine tab and tap Custom: pick a base rhythm, set how long each vibration and pause lasts, test it, and save it. Read how to build your own rhythm.

Does PULSE need any permissions?

No. PULSE asks for no permissions and doesn't use the camera, microphone, location, motion sensors, contacts or health data.

My iPhone gets warm during long sessions.

That's normal: long sessions can warm the phone and use battery. Use the auto-off timer, and take a break whenever you like.
