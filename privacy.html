The short version
PULSE has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect any data about you. Your settings and the custom modes you create are stored only on your iPhone.

This policy explains how PULSE ("the app", "we"), developed by Essential Dev Team, handles information. Our App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What the app stores on your iPhone #

  • Your choices — intensity, speed, auto-off timer, the last mode you used and your appearance setting.
  • Custom modes you create (name and rhythm).
  • A count of completed sessions, used only once to ask whether you'd like to rate the app.

These are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone. They are never sent to us. They are included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of the phone, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Vibration #

Massage patterns are played on your iPhone's Taptic Engine using Apple's Core Haptics. PULSE does not use the camera, microphone, location, motion sensors, contacts or health data, and asks for no permissions.

The only network connections the app makes #

DestinationWhenWhat is sent
Apple App Store (StoreKit)When you view plans, buy, or restore purchasesHandled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details.
itunes.apple.com (App Store lookup)Once when the app opensThe app's bundle identifier only, to check whether a newer version is available. Nothing that identifies you.

No data is sent to us or to any third party.

What we do not collect #

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, advertising identifier (IDFA), vendor identifier (IDFV), usage data or crash data. The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising.

Children #

PULSE is not directed at children under 13. We collect no information from anyone.

Your rights and deleting your data #

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request, export or delete on our side. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Changes #

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and our App Store privacy label will say so before that version is released.
