3. PULSE Pro and in-app purchases #

The free version includes 4 modes, Soft and Medium intensity, and 5-minute sessions. PULSE Pro unlocks every mode including Journey, Hard intensity, longer or unlimited sessions, and custom modes. Pro is sold three ways, all processed by Apple:

  • Weekly — $2.99 per week, auto-renewing, with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers.
  • Yearly — $19.99 per year, auto-renewing.
  • Lifetime — $29.99 once. A one-time purchase that does not renew.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when the free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription. Prices may vary by country and are always shown in the app before you buy. Refunds are handled by Apple. Family Sharing is not available for PULSE Pro.

Lapsed subscribers may sometimes be offered a discounted first year by Apple; the price and renewal terms are shown before purchase. The contents of the free version may change in future versions.
